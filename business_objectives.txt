Core problem: The e-commerce business wants to understand customer purchasing behaviour and sales performance in order to improve customer retention, 
optimize product and marketing strategy, and grow revenue from existing and new customers. 
Leadership needs a clear, data-backed view of who buys, what they buy, when, and how that behaviour translates into revenue and loyalty.

Guiding analytical questions:

1Q.what is the overall revenue trend month-over-month across the two-year period, and which months show seasonal peaks?
2Q.Which products (by StockCode/Description) generate the highest total revenue and the highest total quantity sold?
3Q.Which countries contribute the most revenue, and how concentrated is the business on its home market (UK) versus international customers?
4Q.What is the customer distribution by Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value (RFM), and how many high-value "champion" customers exist versus at-risk/lapsed customers?
5Q.What is the customer retention/repeat-purchase rate — what share of customers make more than one purchase, and how does that vary by acquisition cohort (month of first purchase)?
6Q.What is the average order value (AOV) and how has it trended over time?
7Q.Are there clear day-of-week or time-of-day patterns in order volume that could inform marketing send-times or staffing?
8Q.How significant is the impact of cancelled/returned orders (negative quantities) on net revenue, and which products see the highest cancellation rates?
9Q.Which products are most frequently purchased together (market-basket / cross-sell opportunity)?
10Q.What share of total revenue comes from the top 20% of customers (Pareto/80-20 concentration), and how dependent is the business on this group?
11Q.How does customer lifetime value (CLV, approximated) differ between countries or customer segments?
12Q.What is the typical time gap between a customer's first and second purchase, and does a longer gap predict churn?
13Q.Are wholesale-sized orders (very high quantity, low per-unit price) a meaningfully different segment from typical retail-sized orders, and should they be analyzed/targeted separately?
14Q.What actionable retention, marketing, and merchandising recommendations follow from the RFM segmentation and product-performance analysis?
