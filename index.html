          Key findings

          • Seasonal patterns.Revenue exhibited noticeable fluctuations across different months, indicating seasonal purchasing patterns.
          • Product concentration.A limited number of products generated a significant proportion of total revenue.
          • UK dependence.The United Kingdom contributed the largest share of revenue, demonstrating strong market dependence.
          • Repeat customers matter.Repeat customers played a crucial role in overall sales performance.
          • Revenue concentration.A relatively small percentage of customers generated a large portion of total revenue.
          • Product variance.Product performance varied significantly, highlighting opportunities for inventory optimization and targeted marketing.
